Our story

Compassion is at the heart of everything.

Paws & Hope is a student-created animal shelter website concept. It represents a caring organisation that aims to rescue abandoned and neglected animals, provide basic care and connect them with responsible adopters.

Our approach is built on patience, respect and responsible pet ownership. We want every adoption to be a good match for both the animal and the family.
